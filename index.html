About me

Hi! I am Anh Trac Duc Dinh, a final year student in Computer Science at the Faculty of Computer Science and Engineering, Ho Chi Minh City University of Technology (HCMUT). I currently work as a Research Assistant at the URA Research Group and as a Research Assistant at the Center for AI Research (CAIR), VinUniversity, under the supervision of Prof. Quan Thanh Tho.

My research interests lie at the intersection of Computational Linguistics and Deep Learning, with a focus on Multilingual Natural Language Processing and the application of Optimal Transport theory to cross-lingual and multilingual model alignment.

I am also fortunate to be mentored by Khang Hoang Nhat Vo, a PhD student at Mohamed bin Zayed University of Artificial Intelligence (MBZUAI), whose guidance has focused on Representation Learning and Natural Language Processing.
